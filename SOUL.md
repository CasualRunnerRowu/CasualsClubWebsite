# Casuals Club Soul

## Brand Writeup

Our city has always had a fitness/running culture.
But for a lot of us, something was missing.

Most run groups felt either too serious, too competitive, or catered to an older crowd. If you just wanted to move your body, meet new people, and have a good time, there wasn’t really a space for that.

At the same time, much of the social life in the city revolved around late nights and trouble. There weren’t many events built around health, movement, and genuine connection. It felt like there was room for something different — something social, but also active and energizing.

So a few friends decided to start something of their own.

That idea became Casuals Club.

Casuals Club isn’t just a run club. It’s a community-driven social club built around movement, connection, music, and shared experiences.

The vision started simple: People who want to stay fit, people who just want to socialize, first-time runners, and even those who simply come out for a walk. The community welcomes everyone. It’s never been just about running.

A casual 5K through the city where people go at their own pace, meet new faces, and start their weekend in a positive way. Afterwards, we grab coffee, socialize and make connections. Our weekly runs are about showing up, moving together, and connecting.

But what really makes Casuals Club different are the events.
Once a month, the run turns into something bigger — a full social experience built around movement and community. We bring together music, DJs, coffee, local vendors, and pop-ups to create an atmosphere that feels more like a daytime social than a traditional run club event.

People arrive, music is playing, vendors are setting up, and the energy starts building. We run together, then stay afterwards for coffee, music, dancing, and connecting with the community.

Think of it as a healthy version of a party — movement, music, coffee, and community all in one place.

Because at the end of the day, running is just the starting point.

The real goal is to build a community where people can meet new friends, support local businesses, stay active, and be part of something fun and welcoming.

We’re here to run together, connect, and build something special in the city — one run, one event, and one community at a time.

## Derived Direction

- Culture-first, not performance-first.
- Movement is the entry point; connection is the reason people stay.
- Younger, more social, more welcoming energy.
- Daytime social atmosphere instead of nightlife energy.
- Music, networking, local vendors, and community should appear as strongly as running.
- Coffee can exist, but it should not dominate the identity.
- The site should feel polished, social, modern, and community-driven.
- Avoid naming a specific place on the site; if location is mentioned, keep it generic as "the city."
